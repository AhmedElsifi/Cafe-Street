About us

We are passionate about delivering the finest coffee experience right to your doorstep.

Company information

Cafe Street is a premium coffee brand founded in 2024, born from a deep love for exceptional coffee. What started as a small coffee cart in the heart of the city has evolved into a beloved brand serving thousands of customers daily across multiple locations.

We partner directly with sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Brazil to source the finest Arabica beans. Our expert roasters bring out the unique flavor profiles of each origin, ensuring every cup tells a story of quality and craftsmanship.

With a team of over 50 passionate baristas and coffee enthusiasts, we are committed to redefining the coffee experience one cup at a time. Whether you visit us in-store or order through our delivery service, we guarantee freshness and quality in every sip.

Vision & mission

What we aspire to achieve

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted and beloved coffee brand, recognized for our unwavering commitment to quality, sustainability, and customer satisfaction. We envision a future where every person has access to exceptional coffee that inspires productivity and joy.

Our Mission

To deliver premium, ethically sourced coffee to our customers with speed and care. We strive to create meaningful connections between coffee lovers and the farmers who grow their beans, while maintaining the highest standards of environmental responsibility.
